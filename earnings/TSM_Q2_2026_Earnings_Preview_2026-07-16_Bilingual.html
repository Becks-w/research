Key Metrics to Watch核心关注点（按重要性排序）

  1. Q3'26 revenue guidance / 2H26 trajectory · Q3 营收指引 / 下半年路径
    Management already raised FY26 USD revenue growth guidance to "above 30%" at the Q1 call. Since Q2 is largely de-risked by the June monthly print, the Q3 guide is the single biggest swing factor for the stock.
    管理层已在 Q1 电话会将 FY26 美元营收增速指引上调至"30% 以上"。由于 Q2 已被6月月度数据部分排雷，Q3 指引才是本次财报对股价影响最大的变量。
  2. Gross margin vs. 65.5–67.5% guide, and margin bridge · Gross margin 是否落在指引区间及 margin bridge 表述
    Two known headwinds: N2 (2nm) ramp diluting FY26 GM ~2–3pp; overseas (Arizona) fab dilution ~2–3pp initially, possibly widening to 3–4pp as it scales. Margin at/above the high end despite these signals pricing power.
    两个已知逆风：N2（2nm）爬坡拖累 FY26 GM 约 2–3pp；海外（Arizona）产能拖累约 2–3pp，随产能扩大可能扩至 3–4pp。若 margin 仍达到或超过区间上限，说明提价能力能对冲稀释。
  3. 2026 capex guidance · 2026 年 capex 指引
    Already guided to the high end of $52B–$56B. A further upward revision confirms capital intensity rising with AI demand — bullish signal, but a near-term FCF headwind.
    此前已指引至 $52B–$56B 区间上限。若进一步上调，将确认资本开支强度与 AI 需求同步走高——对长期需求是利好，但短期压制 FCF。
  4. CoWoS / advanced-packaging capacity · CoWoS / 先进封装产能
    Target ~125–130k wafers/month by year-end 2026, up from ~75k at end-2025. This is the binding constraint on AI accelerator shipments (Nvidia, AMD, custom ASICs) across the whole supply chain.
    目标 2026 年底达约 12.5–13 万片/月，较 2025 年底约 7.5 万片/月。这是 AI 加速卡出货（Nvidia、AMD、custom ASIC）的硬约束，牵动整个供应链。
  5. HPC/AI revenue mix · HPC/AI 营收占比
    HPC was 61% of Q1'26 revenue (up from 55% in Q4'25, 51% a year ago). Watch whether concentration keeps climbing, and commentary on customer diversification (Nvidia/AMD vs. hyperscaler custom silicon).
    Q1'26 HPC 占营收 61%（Q4'25 为 55%，去年同期 51%）。关注该集中度是否继续上升，及客户结构多元化（Nvidia/AMD vs. 云厂商 custom silicon）相关表态。
  6. N2 (2nm) ramp commentary · N2（2nm）爬坡进展
    Entered HVM in Q4'25, revenue contribution since March 2026 (Fab 22 Kaohsiung + Fab 20 Hsinchu), ~90–100k wpm combined capacity, both reportedly fully booked for the year. Watch pricing/yield commentary and 2027 capacity plans.
    2025 年 Q4 进入 HVM，自 2026 年 3 月起贡献营收（高雄 Fab 22 + 新竹 Fab 20），合计产能约 9–10 万片/月，据称全年已订满。关注定价/良率表态及 2027 年产能规划。
  7. Smartphone segment trend · 智能手机业务趋势
    Fell 11% QoQ in Q1'26 (seasonal). Watch H2 recovery commentary tied to iPhone/Android build cycles — currently ignored by the market as HPC does the heavy lifting.
    Q1'26 环比下滑 11%（季节性）。关注下半年 iPhone/Android 备货周期带动的复苏表态——目前市场因 HPC 强劲而暂未计价手机端疲软。
  8. Tariff / geopolitical commentary · 关税 / 地缘政治相关表述
    The Apr 27, 2026 US–Taiwan framework ties tariffs to U.S. domestic value-add, not just headline rates. Also watch for new export-control commentary affecting China-bound advanced-node shipments.
    2026年4月27日美台框架将关税待遇与美国本土 value-add 挂钩，而非仅看名义税率。同时关注对华先进制程出口管制相关的新表态。
  9. Pricing power on advanced nodes · 先进制程定价能力
    With N2 capacity fully booked, watch for wafer pricing commentary, particularly framed against Samsung Foundry / Intel Foundry competitive dynamics.
    N2 产能已订满背景下，关注晶圆涨价相关表态，尤其是与 Samsung Foundry / Intel Foundry 竞争格局对比下的定价论述。

Catalyst Checklist催化剂清单

  • Q3'26 revenue guidance vs. buy-side expectations for continued sequential AI-driven growth — matters more than the Q2 beat itself.
    Q3 2026 营收指引 —— 相较买方对 AI 驱动持续环比增长的预期，这一项比 Q2 本身的 beat 更重要。
  • Gross margin print and forward bridge — whether management reiterates or widens the 2–3pp (N2) + 2–3pp (overseas) dilution guardrails.
    Gross margin 实际值及前瞻 margin bridge —— 关注管理层是否重申或扩大 2–3pp（N2）+ 2–3pp（海外）的稀释区间。
  • Capex guidance — any move beyond "high end of $52–56B" reads as a strong AI-capex-cycle signal.
    Capex 指引 —— 若突破"$52–56B 区间上限"的措辞，将被解读为 AI capex 周期强劲的信号。
  • CoWoS capacity commentary — explicit wpm figures and whether the 125–130k year-end target is on track.
    CoWoS 产能相关表态 —— 具体片/月数字，及年底 12.5–13 万片/月目标是否如期。
  • Tariff/export-control language — incremental detail that could reprice geopolitical risk into the stock.
    关税/出口管制相关措辞 —— 可能重新定价股价中地缘政治风险的增量细节。
