Headline: a clean double beat and a guide-up, yet the stock still fell — because it had already priced in near-perfection. Revenue of $5.46B beat consensus (~$5.25B) by ~4%, and came in above the high end of TI's own $5.0-5.4B guide. EPS of $2.14 beat consensus (~$1.93) by ~11%, though management flagged a 5-cent one-time benefit not in the original guidance — the second consecutive quarter with such an item. Q3 guidance of $5.65-6.15B / $2.23-2.57 also beat Street expectations. Yet TXN shares fell roughly 3-4% in the reaction window. The reason isn't the print — it's valuation: TXN entered earnings up ~65-69% year-to-date and trading at ~39x NTM P/E, a rich multiple for a company with a ~3% 10-year revenue CAGR. This review does not change our house SELL, $170 PT (Jul 21 initiation) — the post-earnings pullback is a small, early data point in the direction of our valuation-discipline thesis, though the gap to spot remains very large.

核心结论：业绩和指引"双超"，但股价仍然下跌——因为股价在财报前已经price in了近乎完美的预期。本季度营收 $5.46B，超一致预期（约 $5.25B）约4%，也高于公司自身给出的 $5.0-5.4B 指引区间上限。EPS $2.14，超一致预期（约$1.93）约11%，但管理层披露其中含5美分的一次性利好（未包含在原始指引中）——这已是连续第二个季度出现该类项目。Q3指引 $5.65-6.15B / $2.23-2.57 同样超出街上预期。然而TXN股价在财报后反应窗口内仍下跌约3-4%。原因不在业绩本身，而在估值：TXN财报前年初至今已上涨约65-69%，NTM P/E约39倍，相对其近10年营收CAGR仅约3%的公司而言，估值明显偏贵。本次财报不改变我们此前（7月21日首次覆盖报告）给出的 SELL，目标价$170 评级——财报后的回调是朝我们"估值纪律"逻辑方向迈出的一小步信号，但与现价的差距仍然非常大。

6Read-Through to House Thesis对我方核心观点的印证

This print does not change our SELL, $170 PT from the July 21 initiation report — the underlying franchise-quality and FCF-inflection thesis is intact and, if anything, strengthened (margin expansion, capex discipline, cash generation all executing as modeled). But the SELL call was never a franchise-quality call — it is a valuation call. This quarter is a live illustration of exactly that tension: a genuine beat-and-raise still produced a negative stock reaction, because ~65-69% of pre-print YTD appreciation had already priced in outcomes at least this good. The ~4% pullback moves the stock modestly toward our target but leaves the vast majority of the ~40% valuation gap vs. our $170 PT unresolved.

本次财报不改变我们7月21日首次覆盖报告中给出的 SELL，目标价$170 评级——公司基本面质量与自由现金流拐点逻辑保持完好，甚至进一步得到强化（利润率扩张、资本开支纪律、现金生成均按模型预期兑现）。但SELL评级从来不是对公司基本面质量的否定，而是一个估值层面的判断。本季度恰好是这一矛盾的鲜活例证：即便是真实的"双超+上调指引"，股价仍出现负面反应，原因在于财报前约65-69%的年内涨幅已经price in了不逊于本次业绩的结果。财报后约4%的回调，使股价小幅向我们目标价方向靠拢，但与$170目标价之间约40%的估值差距，绝大部分仍未消化。
