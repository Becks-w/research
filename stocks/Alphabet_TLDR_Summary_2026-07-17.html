Investment Summary

Alphabet delivered FY2025A revenue of $402.8B (+15% YoY) and Q1 FY2026 revenue of $109.9B (+22% YoY), its 11th straight quarter of double-digit growth, led by Google Cloud's 63% YoY acceleration. Gemini 3.1 Pro reportedly leads 12 of 18 industry benchmarks, and Waymo has scaled past 500,000 paid rides/week. Fundamentals are genuinely strong and still improving.

The problem is valuation math, not business quality. Our DCF (unlevered FCF from Task 2, refreshed WACC 10.77%) spans $65–$245/share across three terminal-value methods, driven by how quickly ~37%-of-revenue AI capex normalizes. Our 6-peer comps (MSFT, AMZN, META, AAPL, ORCL, TTD) imply $222–$294/share at peer-median multiples — even though Alphabet already trades at a ~53% premium to that median (21.0x vs 13.75x EV/EBITDA NTM). Weighted (DCF 45% / Comps 55%): $208 base, $210 rounded target.

Why HOLD, not SELL: the ~$220/share gap to Street consensus ($431.72 avg, 53–68 analysts Strong Buy) is explainable, not irrational — it hinges on (1) whether AI capex normalizes toward 15% of revenue (our assumption) or faster, and (2) whether Alphabet's current multiple premium over peers is durable. We don't think our numbers are "wrong" — we think the market may be underwriting a more optimistic resolution to both questions than we're willing to assume. A reader who shares the bullish view on either should reasonably underwrite something closer to $430.

投资摘要

Alphabet FY2025A 营收 $402.8B（+15% YoY），Q1 FY2026 营收 $109.9B（+22% YoY），已连续11个季度实现两位数增长，主要驱动力是 Google Cloud 同比+63%的加速增长。据报道 Gemini 3.1 Pro 在18项行业基准测试中12项排名第一，Waymo 周付费订单已突破50万单。基本面确实强劲且仍在改善。

问题不在于公司质地，而在于估值测算。我们的 DCF（unlevered FCF 沿用 Task 2 模型，WACC 更新为10.77%）在三种 terminal value 方法下测算区间为 每股$65–$245，核心变量是占营收约37%的 AI 资本开支多快回归常态。我们基于6家同业（MSFT、AMZN、META、AAPL、ORCL、TTD）的 comps 分析，以同业组中位数倍数测算隐含 每股$222–$294——尽管 Alphabet 当前实际交易倍数已较该中位数溢价约53%（EV/EBITDA NTM 21.0x vs. 13.75x）。加权（DCF 45% / Comps 55%）：基准值$208，四舍五入目标价$210。

为何是 HOLD 而非 SELL：与卖方一致预期（均值$431.72，53-68位分析师 Strong Buy）约$220/股的差距是可以解释的，而非不合理——核心取决于（1）AI资本开支能否回归至占营收15%（我们的假设）或更快回归，（2）Alphabet 相较同业的当前估值溢价是否具备持久性。我们不认为自己的测算“错了”——我们认为市场对这两个问题可能给出了比我们愿意假设的更乐观的答案。若读者对上述任一问题持更乐观看法，合理地锚定接近$430的估值也是可以理解的。
