1 Setup

  • ASML has beaten and raised guidance twice already this year: Q4 2025 print set FY2026 sales guidance at €34–39bn; Q1 2026 beat consensus (€8.8bn vs. ~€8.6bn expected) and raised FY2026 guidance to €36–40bn, citing customers "accelerating capacity expansion plans" for AI. Source: ASML Q1 2026 press release; StockTitan 6-K filing.
  • Options market is pricing an ~8.4% move (either direction) on the print — roughly 2x the stock's average post-earnings move of ~4% over the last four quarters. Source: TradingKey Q2 2026 preview — third-party derived estimate, can move intraday.
  • Consensus (aggregated via press coverage — not verified against a paid terminal, flag for recheck): Revenue €8.8–9.0bn (~$10.0–10.3bn); EPS ~€6.8–7.0; Gross margin ~51–52%, in line with guidance.
  • This is a "meets vs. beats-and-raises" setup. Two consecutive guidance raises this year have pulled buy-side expectations above the headline guidance range. A quarter that simply lands in-range with unchanged FY26 guidance could be read as a relative disappointment even if it's a "good" quarter in absolute terms.

2 Key Metrics to Watch / 核心关注指标

1. FY2026 guidance — raised again, held, or cut?

1. FY2026 guidance——第三次上调、维持不变，还是下调？

FY26 guide is currently €36–40bn (raised from €34–39bn in April). A third consecutive raise would confirm the AI-capex-driven upcycle is broadening; a "held" guide even with an in-line Q2 beat risks a "priced-for-perfection" selloff given the stock's re-rating YTD.

当前FY26 guidance为€36–40bn（4月由€34–39bn上调而来）。若连续第三次上调，将确认AI资本开支驱动的上行周期正在扩散；若即便Q2 beat，guidance仍维持不变，鉴于股价年初至今已大幅重估，存在"利好出尽"式抛售的风险。

2. China mix and export-control commentary.

2. 中国区收入占比及出口管制相关表态。

China's share of system sales fell from 36% in Q4 2025 to 19% in Q1 2026, with management guiding China to hover around ~20% for FY2026. Watch for: (a) confirmation the 20% run-rate is holding; (b) updated commentary on the pending U.S. MATCH Act (would further tighten DUV/services exports to China); (c) whether lost China DUV revenue is being fully offset by EUV demand from Taiwan/Korea/US customers. Source: press coverage of Q1 2026 results — cross-check against the actual Q2 shareholder letter.

中国系统销售占比已从2025年Q4的36%骤降至2026年Q1的19%，管理层guidance中国区FY2026占比将维持在约20%左右。需关注：(a) 20%这一run-rate是否保持稳定；(b) 管理层对MATCH Act（若通过将进一步收紧对华DUV/服务出口）的最新表态；(c) 中国DUV收入流失是否已被台湾/韩国/美国客户的EUV需求充分对冲。来源：Q1 2026财报相关媒体报道——建议对照Q2实际股东信核实。

3. Bookings / order momentum proxy — now harder to read.

3. 订单/bookings动能替代指标——比以往更难判断。

ASML discontinued quarterly net bookings disclosure starting Q1 2026, removing the market's cleanest forward-demand signal. Listen for commentary on 2H26/2027 delivery cadence, backlog conversion, and customer capacity-expansion language.

ASML自2026年Q1起已停止披露季度净订单数据，市场因此失去了最清晰的前瞻性需求信号。需重点关注管理层关于下半年/2027年交付节奏、backlog转化及客户产能扩张的定性表态。

4. High-NA EUV shipment/qualification progress.

4. High-NA EUV出货与验证进展。

Two High-NA systems shipped in Q4 2025; ASML has guided to ~60 low-NA EUV tool shipments in 2026 (+25% y/y), with High-NA adoption still described as more of a 2027 catalyst. Any slippage in qualification timelines would be a read-through to 2027–2028 revenue, not 2026 numbers.

2025年Q4已出货两台High-NA系统；ASML guidance称2026年将出货约60台low-NA EUV设备（同比+25%），High-NA规模化采用更多被视为2027年的催化剂。验证时间线若延迟，主要影响2027-2028年营收爬坡预期，而非2026年数字。

5. Gross margin trajectory and mix.

5. 毛利率走势及产品结构。

Q1 print was 53.0% gross margin, above the 51–52% Q2 guide, aided by a strong Installed Base Management (service) contribution (~€2.5bn). Watch whether systems mix (EUV vs. DUV) and IBM mix continue to support margins in the guided 51–53% FY26 range.

Q1财报毛利率为53.0%，高于Q2 guidance的51-52%，部分受益于强劲的Installed Base Management（服务业务，约€2.5bn）贡献。需关注系统结构（EUV vs. DUV）及IBM占比，能否继续支撑FY26 guidance的51-53%毛利率区间。

6. Free cash flow / capital returns.

6. 自由现金流（FCF）与资本回报。

Q1 2026 free cash flow was negative €2.6bn, with cash & short-term investments down from €13.3bn to €8.4bn q/q, partly reflecting continued buybacks (€1.1bn in Q1) and dividends. A second consecutive negative FCF quarter is worth flagging, not alarming given the capex/inventory build cycle.

2026年Q1自由现金流为负€2.6bn，现金及短期投资余额环比从€13.3bn降至€8.4bn，部分反映持续的股票回购（Q1回购€1.1bn）及分红。连续第二个季度FCF为负值得关注，但考虑到当前资本开支/库存建设周期，尚不算警讯。

4 Catalyst Checklist / 催化剂清单

  1. FY26 guidance: raised / held / cut — the single largest swing factor given two prior raises this year.
  2. China % of sales and MATCH Act commentary — regulatory overhang is live and unresolved.
  3. Qualitative bookings/demand commentary — listen closely to CEO Christophe Fouquet's and CFO's tone in prepared remarks and Q&A.
  4. Gross margin vs. 51–52% guide — confirms/denies margin resilience through an EUV-heavy shipment mix.
  5. High-NA EUV qualification updates — shapes the 2027–2028 thesis and multiple.
  1. FY26 guidance：上调/维持/下调——鉴于今年已两次上调，这是最大的股价摆动因子。
  2. 中国区收入占比及MATCH Act相关表态——监管不确定性仍未解决。
  3. 定性订单/需求表态——需密切关注CEO Christophe Fouquet及CFO在prepared remarks和Q&A中的措辞语气。
  4. 毛利率相对51–52% guidance的表现——验证EUV占比较高出货结构下的毛利率韧性。
  5. High-NA EUV验证进展更新——决定2027-2028年成长叙事及估值倍数。
